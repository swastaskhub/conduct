Customer Conduct & Suspension Policy

Last updated: June 19, 2026

GWENO Hub is committed to maintaining a professional, safe, and respectful environment for users, customer care representatives, technical personnel, and other individuals involved in operating the platform.

This Policy explains the conduct expected when communicating with GWENO Hub and the actions that may be taken when serious or repeated misconduct occurs.

1. Expected Conduct

Users are expected to communicate clearly, honestly, and respectfully when contacting customer care or any GWENO Hub representative.

  • Communicate respectfully when asking questions or making complaints.
  • Provide accurate information when requesting assistance.
  • Allow reasonable time for support staff to investigate issues.
  • Use official GWENO Hub support channels.
  • Follow reasonable instructions provided during account assistance.

2. Prohibited Conduct

The following conduct is not permitted when communicating with GWENO Hub:

  • Threatening or intimidating staff.
  • Harassing or repeatedly abusing customer care representatives.
  • Using deliberately offensive or abusive language.
  • Sending excessive messages intended to pressure staff.
  • Impersonating GWENO Hub personnel or another user.
  • Attempting to obtain unauthorized access or confidential information.
Serious threats, unlawful activity, deliberate system interference, or conduct that creates a security risk may result in immediate restrictions while the matter is reviewed.

3. Warnings and Suspension

Conduct violations may be reviewed individually. Depending on the nature, seriousness, and frequency of the conduct, GWENO Hub may issue a warning, restrict communication, temporarily suspend an account, or permanently terminate an account.

A first or minor incident may result in a warning. Repeated, severe, or threatening conduct may result in stronger action without a prior warning where appropriate.

4. Appeals

A user whose account has been restricted under this Policy may contact support and request a review. Appeals should explain the circumstances clearly and respectfully.
