Customer Conduct & Cybersecurity Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

This Policy forms part of the GWENO Hub Terms & Conditions. It establishes standards for respectful communication, responsible platform use, account security, and conduct that may threaten GWENO Hub, its users, staff, systems, or services.

1. Respectful Communication

Users must communicate respectfully with GWENO Hub customer care, technical teams, administrators, agents, and other users. Complaints and disagreements are permitted, but threats, intimidation, harassment, abusive conduct, and deliberate disruption of support services are not.

2. Prohibited Cybersecurity Conduct

Users must not compromise the confidentiality, integrity, availability, or security of GWENO Hub systems, accounts, information, payment processes, or users.

  • Unauthorized access or attempted unauthorized access;
  • Account takeover or credential theft;
  • Identity theft or impersonation;
  • Phishing or fraudulent communications;
  • Malware, malicious code, or harmful software;
  • Unauthorized collection or extraction of data;
  • Interference with systems or platform availability;
  • Manipulation of tasks, balances, or payment processes;
  • Unauthorized bots, scripts, exploits, or automated abuse; and
  • Attempts to bypass security or verification controls.

Users must not attempt to obtain another person's password, PIN, OTP, authentication information, or other confidential security credentials.

3. Account Security

Users are responsible for protecting their account credentials. GWENO Hub will not request passwords, PINs, OTP codes, or login credentials through unofficial communication channels.

Suspected account compromise, phishing, impersonation, or unauthorized access should be reported promptly through official GWENO Hub support channels.

4. Investigation and Enforcement

GWENO Hub may investigate suspicious activity where reasonably necessary to protect users, systems, payments, or platform integrity. Depending on the circumstances, action may include a warning, restriction, account verification, suspension, withdrawal review, or permanent termination.

Where conduct may constitute unlawful activity, GWENO Hub may preserve relevant information and cooperate with competent authorities where legally required or permitted.

5. Appeals

Users may request a review of a restriction or suspension through official support channels. Appeals are assessed individually based on available information and applicable policies.

Users must continue to communicate respectfully throughout the appeal process.

6. International Compliance

Because GWENO Hub may serve users in multiple countries, users are responsible for complying with applicable laws in their jurisdiction. Nothing in this Policy is intended to prevent the application of mandatory legal rights or protections that cannot lawfully be excluded.

Customer Care
Conduct Policy

Last updated: June 19, 2026 ~2 min read
This is a standalone supplement to the Business Hub Terms & Conditions. It sets out how users are expected to communicate with our customer care team and what happens when that expectation isn't met.
01 Why This Policy Exists

Customer care representatives help users with account activation, task questions, withdrawal verification, and general support. To keep this support sustainable and safe for staff, all users are required to communicate respectfully, regardless of how frustrating an issue may feel.

02 Expected Conduct
Communicate clearly and respectfully, even when raising a complaint.
Allow representatives reasonable time to investigate and respond to issues.
Use official support channels only.
03 Prohibited Conduct

The following are not permitted when interacting with customer care:

  • Rude, hostile, or disrespectful language
  • Threats or intimidation directed at staff
  • Abusive, profane, or harassing messages
  • Repeated, excessive contact intended to pressure staff
04 Consequences

Violations of this policy are reviewed on a case-by-case basis and may result in:

First instance

A formal warning is issued and noted on the account.

Repeated or severe

Account suspension pending review, which may delay task access and withdrawals.

Accounts that repeatedly or severely violate this policy risk permanent termination.
05 Appeals

A user whose account has been suspended under this policy may submit an appeal through official support channels. Appeals are reviewed individually, and reinstatement is at the platform's discretion.

Respectful communication during an appeal is itself part of how the appeal is assessed.
