Customer Conduct & Suspension Policy

Last updated: June 19, 2026

This policy explains the standards expected when users communicate with GWENO Hub customer care, support representatives, staff, agents, and other platform users.

GWENO Hub is committed to providing a professional, safe, and respectful environment for everyone using the platform.

1. Expected Conduct

Users are expected to communicate respectfully and responsibly, including when reporting complaints, payment issues, technical problems, account restrictions, or disagreements.

  • Communicate clearly and respectfully;
  • Provide accurate information when requesting support;
  • Allow reasonable time for an issue to be investigated;
  • Use official GWENO Hub support channels; and
  • Follow reasonable instructions provided by support representatives.

2. Prohibited Conduct

The following conduct is not permitted when interacting with GWENO Hub staff, representatives, or other users:

  • Threats or intimidation;
  • Harassment or abusive behavior;
  • Repeated offensive or profane communication;
  • Discriminatory or hateful communication;
  • Attempts to pressure or intimidate staff into taking unauthorized action;
  • Repeated excessive contact intended to disrupt support operations; or
  • Impersonating GWENO Hub staff or another user.

3. Account Restrictions

Conduct that violates this policy may result in action being taken against the relevant account. The response will depend on the nature, seriousness, frequency, and circumstances of the conduct.

A first or less serious incident may result in a warning. Repeated, serious, threatening, fraudulent, or abusive conduct may result in temporary suspension or permanent termination of the account.

4. Effect of Suspension

During a suspension, access to some or all platform features may be restricted while the matter is reviewed. Depending on the circumstances, task access, account features, communications, or withdrawal processing may be temporarily affected.

5. Appeals

A user whose account has been restricted under this policy may contact GWENO Hub through official support channels to request a review. Appeals are considered based on the available information and relevant circumstances.

Users should remain respectful throughout the appeal process. Additional abusive or threatening communication may be considered when reviewing an appeal.

6. Enforcement

GWENO Hub may take immediate protective action where necessary to protect staff, users, systems, or the integrity of the platform. Nothing in this policy prevents GWENO Hub from taking additional action where required to protect its users, services, or legitimate interests.
